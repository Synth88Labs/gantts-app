What’s new

gantts.app keeps getting better. Here’s what we’ve shipped recently — every update free, no sign up, and running entirely in your browser. We build a lot of this straight from user suggestions.

Got an idea? Tell us what would make your planning easier and we’ll look at building it. Email synth88labs@gmail.com.

August 2026 — Planning & analytics toolkit

Eight additions to the editor, aimed at planning intelligence rather than just drawing bars:

  • Deadlines & slip alerts — set a target date per task; the bar turns red when the schedule blows past it.
  • Tags & filtering — label tasks and filter the whole plan by name, owner or tag.
  • Colour by owner, tag or phase — recolour the bars, with a legend, to read the plan at a glance.
  • Status (RAG) — an automatic On-track / Behind / At-risk column.
  • Key-date markers — labelled vertical lines for launches, gates and quarter ends.
  • Shift the whole plan — move every task at once to start on a new date.
  • Year zoom — a year-scale timeline for multi-quarter programmes.
  • Embeddable charts — a read-only iframe snippet to drop a live chart into any web page.

August 2026 — A bigger template library

Five new ready-made plans, each fully translated into all six languages: Study Plan, Moving House Timeline, Kitchen Remodel Timeline, Crowdfunding Campaign Plan and Performance Review Cycle — bringing the library to over 100 free templates.

The essentials — always in, always free

Everything the editor is built on, with no account and no paywall: task dependencies (finish-to-start and three more), an auto-calculated critical path, an earned-value S-curve, baselines and variance tracking, a working calendar with weekends and holidays, workload and over-allocation, present mode, a command palette, full keyboard control, dark mode, and export to PDF, PNG, Excel, PowerPoint, CSV, MS Project and Mermaid — in six languages.
