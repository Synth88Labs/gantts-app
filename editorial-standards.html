Editorial standards

gantts.app publishes guides and templates that people rely on to plan real work. These are the principles behind that content: who writes it, how we keep it accurate, how the site stays free, and the lines we will not cross.

Who writes gantts.app

The guides and templates are written by Uttam Regmi, founder of Synth88 Labs and the person who builds the editor itself. He is an auditor by profession and has been building software since 2010, so the planning advice here comes from someone who uses schedules in real work, not from an anonymous content desk.

How we research and write

Every guide is written from hands-on use of the actual gantts.app editor. When an article shows an example, a set of dependencies, or a critical path, it was built and checked in the tool before we published it, so the steps match what you will actually see on screen. We write in plain language, explain the why rather than just the how, and we do not generate filler text to hit a word count.

How we keep guides current

Each guide carries a visible date. We revise articles when the tool changes, when search behaviour shows people are asking something we did not answer, or when we simply find a clearer way to explain it, rather than on an artificial refresh schedule. If a screenshot or a step falls out of date, that is a bug we want to fix.

How gantts.app stays free

gantts.app is a lightweight static web app with no expensive backend to run, which is what makes free possible. A single discreet sponsorship slot inside the app helps cover the modest costs. There is no paywall, no locked feature, no account, and, because your plans never leave your browser, nothing about your data to sell. You can read the full detail on the privacy page.

What we will not do

  • No fabricated reviews or star ratings. If we ever show ratings, they will come from real users.
  • No paid placement dressed up as editorial. A recommendation in a guide is there because we think it is genuinely the best option.
  • No dark patterns, fake countdowns, or manufactured urgency.
  • No scraping or republishing other people's content as our own. The guides here are written from scratch.
Spotted an error? Accuracy matters to us. If something in a guide is wrong or out of date, email synth88labs@gmail.com and we will fix it.
